# Fraud and Scam Protection Guide

## What the bank will never ask
Bank Seri Andalas will never ask customers for their password, PIN, one-time password (OTP) or full card number by phone call, SMS, email or social media message. The bank will never send SMS messages containing clickable links.

## Reporting suspected fraud
If you suspect fraud on your account, call the 24-hour fraud hotline at 1-300-88-3728 immediately. You can also use the Kill Switch in the Andalas mobile app to instantly freeze all your cards and block online banking access.

## Kill Switch
The Kill Switch freezes all debit and credit cards and suspends mobile and online banking for the customer. Once activated, the Kill Switch can only be reversed by visiting a branch with your identity card, or by video verification with the contact centre.

## Cooling-off period for new devices
When the Andalas mobile app is registered on a new device, a 12-hour cooling-off period applies. During the cooling-off period, transfers to new recipients are blocked, but transfers to existing saved recipients are allowed.

## Transaction alerts
Customers receive an in-app notification and SMS for every card transaction and every transfer above RM100. Customers cannot switch off alerts for transactions above RM1,000.

## Mule accounts
Allowing another person to use your bank account to receive or transfer money is an offence. Accounts identified as mule accounts are frozen and reported to the authorities.

## Common scams
Common scams include impersonation of bank or government officers, fake investment schemes promising high returns, and parcel delivery scams with malicious links. If someone pressures you to act urgently or to keep the conversation secret, it is likely a scam.
