# Customer Complaints Handling Policy

## How to make a complaint
Customers can lodge a complaint through the Andalas mobile app under Help, by email to complaints@seriandalas.example, through the 24-hour contact centre at 1-300-88-2637, or in person at any branch. Every complaint is assigned a complaint reference number, which the customer should quote in all follow-up communication.

## Acknowledgement
The bank acknowledges every complaint within 2 business days of receipt.

## Resolution timeline
The bank aims to resolve complaints within 14 business days of receipt. Complex complaints that require investigation with third parties, such as card network disputes, may take up to 30 business days. The customer is informed in writing if the resolution will take longer than 14 business days, with the reason for the delay.

## Unauthorised transaction disputes
For disputes about unauthorised card transactions, the customer should report the transaction as soon as possible and no later than 60 days from the statement date. A temporary credit may be given for the disputed amount while the investigation is ongoing.

## Escalation
If the customer is not satisfied with the outcome, they may request a review by the Complaints Review Unit, which is independent of the business unit that handled the original complaint. If the customer remains dissatisfied after the review, they may refer the dispute to the external financial ombudsman scheme within 6 months of the bank's final decision.

## Record keeping
All complaints and their outcomes are recorded and reviewed monthly by the Customer Experience and Conduct team to identify recurring issues and root causes.
