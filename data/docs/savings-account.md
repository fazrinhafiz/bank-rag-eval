# Andalas eSavings Account - Product Terms

## Opening an account
The eSavings account can be opened fully online through the Andalas mobile app using electronic Know-Your-Customer verification. Applicants must be at least 18 years old. The minimum initial deposit is RM20. Each customer may hold a maximum of 2 eSavings accounts.

## Interest rates
Interest is calculated daily and credited monthly on the last business day of the month. Balances up to RM10,000 earn 0.25% per annum. The portion of the balance between RM10,000 and RM50,000 earns 1.5% per annum. The portion of the balance above RM50,000 earns 2.0% per annum.

## Minimum balance
There is no minimum balance requirement and no monthly service fee for the eSavings account.

## Withdrawals and transfers
Customers can make unlimited free instant transfers to other local bank accounts through DuitNow in the mobile app. ATM withdrawals at Bank Seri Andalas ATMs are free.

## Dormant accounts
An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 12 consecutive months is classified as dormant. Dormant accounts cannot be used for transfers until reactivated. To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must verify their identity at a branch or through video verification in the app. Balances in accounts that remain dormant for 7 years are transferred to the government's unclaimed moneys registry as required by law.

## Joint accounts
The eSavings account is available as an individual account only. Joint accounts are not supported for eSavings.

## Closing an account
Customers can close the account in the mobile app at any time. There is no account closure fee if the account has been open for more than 3 months. Accounts closed within 3 months of opening incur a closure fee of RM10.
