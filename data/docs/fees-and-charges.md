# Schedule of Fees and Charges (Retail)

## ATM withdrawals
Cash withdrawal at Bank Seri Andalas ATMs is free. Cash withdrawal at other local banks' ATMs costs RM1.00 per transaction. Cash withdrawal at overseas ATMs costs RM12.00 per transaction plus a currency conversion charge of 1% of the transaction amount.

## Fund transfers
DuitNow and instant transfers through the mobile app are free for amounts up to RM5,000 per transaction. Transfers above RM5,000 cost RM0.50 per transaction. Interbank GIRO transfers cost RM0.10 per transaction. Telegraphic transfers overseas cost RM30.00 per transaction plus correspondent bank charges.

## Debit card
The annual fee for the Andalas debit card is RM8.00. A replacement debit card costs RM12.00. Debit card transactions in foreign currency incur a currency conversion charge of 1% of the transaction amount.

## Statements and documents
Electronic statements are free. A request for a printed copy of a past statement costs RM5.00 per statement month. A bank confirmation letter costs RM20.00 per letter.

## Cheques
A cheque book of 25 leaves costs RM10.00, plus stamp duty of RM0.15 per cheque. A returned cheque due to insufficient funds incurs a charge of RM150.00 per cheque.

## Counter services
Cash deposits at the branch counter are free. Coin deposits of more than 100 coins incur a handling charge of 1% of the coin value, subject to a minimum of RM2.00.
