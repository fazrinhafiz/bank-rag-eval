# Andalas Platinum Credit Card - Product Terms

## Annual fee
The principal card carries an annual fee of RM250. The annual fee is waived for the following year if the cardholder makes at least 12 retail transactions of any amount within the card anniversary year. Supplementary cards are free of annual fee for life. Up to 5 supplementary cards can be issued per principal account.

## Finance charges
Finance charges are tiered based on the cardholder's repayment record over the last 12 months. Tier 1 cardholders who paid the minimum payment on time for 12 consecutive months are charged 13.5% per annum. Tier 2 cardholders who paid on time for 10 or 11 months are charged 15% per annum. Tier 3 cardholders are charged 18% per annum. Finance charges are calculated daily on the outstanding balance from the transaction posting date.

## Interest-free period
Retail purchases enjoy an interest-free period of up to 20 days from the statement date, provided the full outstanding balance is paid by the payment due date. The interest-free period does not apply to cash advances or balance transfers.

## Minimum monthly payment
The minimum monthly payment is 5% of the total outstanding balance or RM50, whichever is higher. Any amount above the credit limit and any overdue amounts are added to the minimum payment.

## Late payment charge
If the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date, a late payment charge of 1% of the total outstanding balance is imposed, subject to a minimum of RM10 and a maximum of RM100.

## Cash advance
Cash advances can be withdrawn at any ATM displaying the card network logo. The cash advance fee is 5% of the amount withdrawn or RM25, whichever is higher. Cash advances attract a finance charge of 18% per annum, calculated daily from the withdrawal date until full repayment, regardless of tier.

## Credit limit
The minimum credit limit is RM3,000. A cardholder may request a temporary credit limit increase for up to 60 days through the Andalas mobile app. Over-limit transactions are declined by default unless the cardholder opts in to over-limit processing.

## Rewards
Cardholders earn 1 Andalas Point for every RM1 spent on retail transactions. Points are not earned on cash advances, balance transfers, fees or government payments. Andalas Points expire 3 years after the month in which they were earned.

## Lost or stolen card
Report a lost or stolen card immediately through the 24-hour contact centre at 1-300-88-2637 or freeze the card in the Andalas mobile app. The cardholder's liability for unauthorised transactions made before the loss is reported is capped at RM250, provided the cardholder has not acted fraudulently or with gross negligence. A replacement card fee of RM12 applies.
