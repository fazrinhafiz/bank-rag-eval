# Andalas Personal Financing - Product Terms

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Malaysian citizens aged between 21 and 60 years old at the time of application. The minimum gross monthly income is RM3,000. Applicants must have been in their current employment for at least 6 months, or have been self-employed with a registered business for at least 2 years.

## Financing amount and tenure
The financing amount ranges from RM5,000 to RM150,000, subject to credit assessment. The financing tenure ranges from 2 years to 7 years. The maximum financing amount is also capped at 10 times the applicant's gross monthly income.

## Profit rate
The flat profit rate starts from 6.5% per annum. The final rate offered depends on the applicant's credit score, income and existing commitments. The effective annual rate is disclosed in the Product Disclosure Sheet before acceptance.

## Required documents
Salaried applicants must submit a copy of their identity card, the latest 3 months of salary slips and the latest 3 months of bank statements. Self-employed applicants must submit business registration documents and the latest 6 months of business bank statements.

## Approval and disbursement
Applications submitted through the Andalas mobile app with complete documents receive a decision within 2 business days. Approved financing is disbursed into the customer's Andalas savings account within 1 business day after acceptance of the offer.

## Fees and charges
A stamp duty of 0.5% of the financing amount is payable and is deducted from the disbursed amount. There is no processing fee. A late payment charge of 1% per annum on the overdue instalment amount is imposed for each day the instalment remains unpaid.

## Early settlement
Customers may settle the financing early at any time. Early settlement within the first 3 years of the financing tenure incurs an early settlement fee of 3% of the outstanding financing amount. No early settlement fee applies after 3 years. A rebate on unearned profit is given upon early settlement.

## Debt consolidation
Personal financing may be used to consolidate credit card balances. For debt consolidation, the bank pays the other card issuers directly and the customer must cancel the consolidated cards within 30 days.
