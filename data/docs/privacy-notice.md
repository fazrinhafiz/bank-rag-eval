# Personal Data Privacy Notice

## Personal data we collect
We collect personal data that you provide when you apply for or use our products, including your name, identity card number, contact details, employment and income information, and transaction records. We also collect device information when you use the Andalas mobile app.

## Purposes of processing
We process personal data to assess applications, operate accounts, prevent fraud and money laundering, comply with legal and regulatory requirements, handle complaints, and, with your consent, send marketing communications.

## Disclosure
We may disclose personal data to credit reporting agencies, regulators and law enforcement agencies where required by law, our appointed service providers under confidentiality obligations, and other companies within the Seri Andalas Group. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

## Retention
We retain personal data for 7 years after the end of the customer relationship, or longer where required by law or for ongoing legal proceedings.

## Your rights
You may request access to, or correction of, your personal data by submitting the Data Access Request Form at any branch or by emailing dpo@seriandalas.example. Data access requests are processed within 21 days. A fee of RM10 may be charged for each data access request.

## Marketing preferences
You can withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time in the Andalas mobile app under Settings, Privacy. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect service messages such as transaction alerts and statements.

## Cross-border transfer
Personal data may be stored or processed outside Malaysia by our appointed cloud service providers, subject to contractual safeguards that require an equivalent level of protection.
