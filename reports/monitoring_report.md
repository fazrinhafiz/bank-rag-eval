## Reading the result

- 224 of 500 drifted-window requests were surge questions. The pipeline abstained on 148 (66%) of them, which is what pushes the abstention rate up.
- It **answered the other 76** from the nearest covered section (personal-loan: 51, credit-card: 23, savings-account: 2). Those answers cite real policy text, but it is the wrong product. This is the same failure the offline eval shows on una-03 / una-08 / una-10. Drift monitoring is the production safety net for out-of-scope questions that the abstain threshold lets through.
- The pii_rate change (16.8% -> 6.6%) is a **mix effect**, not a privacy event: the surge questions contain no PII, so the share of PII-bearing requests is diluted. A real alert triage would note it and close it.
- Recommended response: route home-loan and fixed-deposit intents to a human or a static 'not available in this channel' reply, and raise a change request to add the mortgage product terms to the knowledge base. Re-run the quality gate after ingesting them.
